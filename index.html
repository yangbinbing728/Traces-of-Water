About

I started Water Traces by noticing the marks water leaves in everyday life: a spill on a table, wet footprints, tears on a face, and rain hitting the ground.

This website brings those moments together. You can explore each kind of mark, make your own composition, and add it to a shared collection.

The collection grows with the people who visit. Each published trace has its own page, so its creator can return to it and share it with someone else.
